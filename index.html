Who we are

About the firm

Lifetime Engineering Solution Pvt. Ltd. is a private firm registered under the Companies Act of Nepal on BS 2072/12/08. We plan, survey, design, supervise and manage projects across all fields of engineering and related science for government, non-government and private clients, with care for the environment and the communities around every project.

Our objectives

What we do

Our fields of specialisation

Consultancy services from the first survey through design and tender documents to construction supervision.

Hydropower and transmission lines

  • Feasibility studies for small hydropower projects
  • Inventories and detailed design of micro hydropower schemes
  • Electrification feasibility studies
  • Detailed survey and design of transmission and distribution lines

Hydromechanical engineering

  • Mechanical design calculations for hydraulic gates, stoplogs, trashracks and hoisting systems
  • Mechanical and hydraulic design calculations for hydromechanical equipment such as bell-mouths, pressure pipes and accessories, expansion joints and bifurcations
  • Detailed design of hydromechanical equipment
  • Tender drawings for hydromechanical equipment

Roads, highways and bridges

  • Transportation planning studies
  • Detailed survey, design and cost estimates for roads, bridges and related structures
  • Quality control during construction supervision
  • Pavement condition evaluation and maintenance planning
  • Site selection, environmental studies and geotechnical investigation

Water supply and sewerage

  • Survey and design of rural water supply and sanitation systems
  • Construction supervision and post-construction evaluation
  • Financial viability studies of water supply and sanitation schemes

Water resources assessment

  • Engineering and agro-socio-economic studies
  • Field surveys
  • Hydrological data collection and analysis
  • Water resources assessment
  • Resource identification and development

Irrigation and drainage

  • Planning and system design
  • Estimation of crop water requirements and drainage runoff
  • Detailed design of canals, embankments and flow control structures, including headworks, weirs, sedimentation basins and sluicing facilities
  • Operation and maintenance programmes
  • Water management and socio-economic baseline surveys

Land survey and mapping

  • Topographical surveys and site maps for roads, bridge sites, irrigation command areas and building sites
  • Infrastructure alignments for canals, drains and roads
  • Foundation stability and settlement monitoring for major structures

Energy

  • Feasibility and planning studies
  • Detailed engineering survey, design and cost estimates for alternative energy sources, including micro hydro, small hydro and biomass

Valuation

  • Valuation of land and buildings for banking and other purposes

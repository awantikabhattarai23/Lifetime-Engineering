HOW TO PUT THE WEBSITE ONLINE WITH A WORKING QUOTE FORM (free)
1. Unzip this file. You will get a folder named lifetime-site containing index.html.
2. Go to app.netlify.com/drop and drag the lifetime-site folder onto the page.
3. Netlify gives you a live link. In Site configuration > Domain management you can add your own domain.
4. For emails: open your site > Forms > Form notifications > Add notification > Email notification,
   and enter lifetimeengineering2021@gmail.com (add Ltesolns@gmail.com and pujangautam5@gmail.com the same way if wanted).
Every quote request is then emailed to you and also saved in the Forms tab.
